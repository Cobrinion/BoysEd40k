# Suppressor Squad — completion review

## Source checked

`Codex (9e) - Space Marines (Errata 1.1).pdf`, Suppressor Squad (printed page 168) and its 100-point fixed squad cost (printed points page 206).

## Implemented

- Fixed squad of two Suppressors and one Sergeant, with the source’s 100-point total.
- Accelerator autocannons, bolt pistols, frag/krak grenades, Jump Infantry movement, Deep Strike approximation and Sergeant profile.
- `Suppressing Fire` is a reusable shared rule, preventing an Infantry target hit by an accelerator autocannon from firing Overwatch that turn.

## Balance check

The closest Firstborn role comparison is a jump-pack Devastator-style fire-support unit rather than an Assault Squad: the squad is only three W1 bodies, but brings nine S7 shots at 48 inches plus suppression.  Its fixed 100-point cost is deliberately below an equivalently armed Firstborn heavy-weapon package, offset by small squad size and no weapon choices.

## Intentional Boys Edition divergence

The 9th-edition source uses M12/W2/BS3+.  The card follows the established normal-Primaris M7/W1/BS4 convention while retaining its jump-pack movement role and fixed three-model source composition.
