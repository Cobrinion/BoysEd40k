# Eliminator Squad — completion review

## Source checked

`Codex (9e) - Space Marines (Errata 1.1).pdf`, Eliminator Squad (printed page 174) and points list (printed page 206).

## Implemented

- Fixed three-model Heavy Support squad: two Eliminators plus exactly one Sergeant, at 30 points per model.
- Mandatory bolt pistol, frag/krak grenades and shared Camo Cloaks; the cloak selection has a min/max which increments exactly with the number of Eliminators, including the Sergeant.
- The two regular Eliminators each choose a bolt sniper rifle or a +10-point las fusil. The Sergeant independently chooses bolt sniper rifle, instigator bolt carbine, or +10-point las fusil.
- The three distinct bolt-sniper rounds, their range/strength/AP roles and their source effects have been ported into the 7th-style weapon profile text.
- Boys Edition standard Primaris profile applied (M7, W1, BS4); Sergeant has A3/Ld9.

## Balance check

The Firstborn Scout Sniper Squad is the closest in-faction benchmark for stealthy character hunting; Devastators are the benchmark for the las-fusil option.  A 90-point base unit gains accurate anti-character fire and robust cover saves, but remains only three W1 3+ bodies without a transport or special-weapon saturation.  The 10-point las-fusil surcharge is in line with the project’s Firstborn heavy-weapon bands.

## Intentional Boys Edition divergence

The 9th-edition source profile is M6/W2/BS2+. This catalogue correctly uses the project’s normal-Primaris M7/W1/BS4 baseline. Its modern precision/mortal-wound effects are expressed through existing 7th-style Sniper, Rending, Ignores Cover and Blast language.
