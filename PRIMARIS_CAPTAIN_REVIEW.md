## Firstborn comparison and balance

The actual current Firstborn Captain is 90 points, WS6/BS5/A3/W3/Ld10; Primaris is 120, WS6/BS6/A4/W3/Ld10. Both have broad gear access, but accuracy/Attacks are not identical. Gravis's existing T5/W4/M5 and Phobos's custom cloak are preserved, not new stat conversions. Existing prices and custom Instigator Brutal (2) need manual balance review; no full-game equivalence is claimed.
