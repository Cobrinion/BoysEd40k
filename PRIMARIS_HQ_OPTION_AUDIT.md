# Primaris HQ option audit - 13 September 2026

Superseded by PRIMARIS_HQ_COMPLETION_REVIEW.md: the five-entry pass and screenshot
error cleanup are implemented. This file records the earlier findings, not the
current list of missing units/options. Optional modern Chaplain litanies still
need an explicit native-system decision.

Compared the present Captain, Lieutenant and Librarian structures with the local
9th-edition Codex datasheets. Existing Boys Edition breadth takes precedence;
modern chapter keywords and restricted loadouts are not automatically imposed.

## Corrected in this pass

- Captain Relic Shield was incorrectly inside the exactly-two-weapons group.
  A sword + heavy bolt pistol + shield therefore counted as three choices and
  could not be selected legally. Moved the same shield entry, ID, price and
  custom rules into Special Issue Wargear. It no longer consumes a weapon slot.
- Captain Heavy Bolt Pistol had an unrelated cost repeat counting Eradicator
  models. Removed that copied modifier; its existing price remains 5 points.
- No Captain weapon profiles, armour costs or expanded armoury were narrowed.

## Confirmed outstanding work - not implemented here

- Lieutenant: the four generic Tacticus loadouts are present, but Phobos and
  Reiver platform/loadout routes are absent. Source Phobos needs master-crafted
  occulus carbine, paired blades and deployment; Reiver needs its special-issue
  pistol, combat knife and Terror Troops. Reuse existing shared mechanics after
  checking the complete relevant units and native armour rules.
- Librarian: Tacticus route and native discipline choice exist. Phobos route,
  camo cloak and Concealed Positions are absent. Do not import modern cast
  counts, mastery levels or new psychic disciplines without native conversion.
- Captain: retain deliberate two-weapon custom armoury. The source's rifle +
  pistol + optional sword package still needs a considered sidearm/slot policy;
  raising the cap to three would also permit three main weapons and is not a
  safe fix. Gravis/Phobos need complete branch-specific source-option checks.
- Primaris Chaplain and Techmarine are absent as units, not merely missing
  equipment options. Do not mark the generic HQ range complete.
- Dark Angels/Space Wolves exclusive carbine, axe and plasma-pistol routes are
  chapter-specific material, distinct from missing generic options. Their
  absence is not a reason to indiscriminately unlock them on every core unit.

This is a bounded audit, not a completed HQ pass. The earlier Lieutenant and
Librarian completion notes cover their implemented Tacticus routes only.
Guilliman and the other HQ entries were not fully source-audited in this pass.
