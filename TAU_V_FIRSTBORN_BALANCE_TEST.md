# Tau vs Firstborn analytical balance test

Status: **no immediate points changes recommended**. The matchup is directionally healthy in an unbuffed shooting comparison, but needs New Recruit roster smoke tests and tabletop games before points move.

## Method

This compares the current catalogue profiles in an unbuffed baseline state. Tau does not use Markerlights, Ethereal invocations, Commander buffs, or drone-controller effects. Firstborn does not use Legion rules, character auras, special ammunition, reactions, cover, or psychic powers.

Targets use the current catalogue baselines:

- Firstborn line marine: T4, 3+ save, BS4, 7&quot; movement.
- Tau Fire Warrior: T3, 4+ save, BS3, 6&quot; movement.
- Tau Riptide: T6, 5W, 2+ save.
- Firstborn Contemptor: T7, 6W, 2+/5++, BS5.

## Core infantry exchange

| Fire Warrior, rapid fire | 15&quot; | 0.22 Firstborn marine | Good massed fire, but vulnerable if Firstborn reaches charge range. |
| Firstborn marine, boltgun | 24&quot; | 0.22 Fire Warrior | Firstborn matches Tau rapid-fire lethality at longer effective safety because of better BS and Tau&apos;s weaker save. |
| Firstborn marine, rapid fire | 12&quot; | 0.44 Fire Warriors | Tau infantry should not trade at close range without numbers, cover, or support. |
| Kroot Carnivore, Kroot rifle | 24&quot; | 0.08 Firstborn marine before Sniper effects | Kroot are correctly a screen/objective unit rather than a marine-killer. |

Conclusion: Fire Warriors are not overpowered in a direct exchange. Their S5, 30&quot; weapon is offset by BS3, T3, 4+ armour, weak melee, and lower leadership. Firstborn should win a close-range or assault-led engagement; Tau should win through range control and support.

## Balance risks specific to this matchup

1. **Markerlights:** Tau&apos;s unbuffed output is acceptable. Multiple markerlight hits can sharply improve it, so every table test needs a "with Markerlights" and "without Markerlights" version.
2. **Riptide durability:** A 2+ T6, 5W model demands dedicated AP2, melta, graviton, and melee testing. Bolter-only tests are not meaningful.
3. **Firstborn special rules:** Fury of the Firstborn, Legion traits, reactions, and character auras may raise Firstborn performance substantially. The Tau catalogue must be tested against representative Legion lists, not only generic tactical marines.
4. **Crisis hardpoints:** The new combined maximum of three equipment selections per Crisis suit is a roster rule that must be confirmed in New Recruit before points testing.

## Recommended matched games

Use 1,500 points, the same mission and terrain, and play each pairing twice with first turn swapped:

1. Fire Warrior, Pathfinder, Devilfish, and Hammerhead core versus Tactical, Rhino, Predator, and Heavy Support core.
2. Crisis/Riptide package versus Contemptor, Terminators, and anti-tank Firstborn support.
3. Tau with normal Markerlight support versus the same Tau list with Markerlights omitted, against the same Firstborn opponent.

Record victory points, shooting phases survived, unit losses by role, and whether a unit&apos;s points felt fair. Adjust points only after at least six matched games.
