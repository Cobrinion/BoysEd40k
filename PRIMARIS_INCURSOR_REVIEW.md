# Incursor Squad — completion review

- 5–10 models: 4–9 Incursors and one Sergeant, at 21 points per model.
- W1 Primaris baseline, matching catalogue convention; Sergeant A3/Ld9.
- Standard wargear is shown as Bolt Pistol, shared Close Combat Weapon, Paired Combat Blades and Occulus Bolt Carbine.
- Paired Combat Blades are presented as their distinct extra-attack wargear effect; they no longer redefine the shared Close Combat Weapon's profile.
- One Haywire Mine may be selected for 10 points. Unit rules remain in the Unit profile's Special Rules characteristic; Concealed Positions and Multi-spectrum Array resolve from shared GST definitions.

Closest Firstborn comparison: Scouts and forward-deploying Tactical elements. Incursors pay for forward deployment, anti-cover shooting and a modest melee edge, but remain W1, lack heavy/special weapon access and are less flexible than Veterans.
