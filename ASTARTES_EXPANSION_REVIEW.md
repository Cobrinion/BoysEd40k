# Astartes expansion — review notes

Date: 29 August 2026

## Added selections

### Adeptus Astartes — Firstborn

- Scout Squad — Troops, 5–10 models.
- Centurion Assault Squad — Elites, 3–6 models.
- Centurion Devastator Squad — Heavy Support, 3–6 models.
- Legion Vindicator Squadron — Heavy Support.
- Legion Whirlwind Squadron — Heavy Support.

The existing `Legion Land Raider Squadron` was retained.  It already provided a
more complete project-native Land Raider implementation, so a second Firstborn
entry would only duplicate it.

### Chaos Space Marines

- Possessed — Elites, 5–10 models.
- Plague Marine Squad — Troops, 5–10 models.
- Obliterators — Heavy Support, 1–3 models.
- Chaos Land Raider — Heavy Support, AV14/14/14, 4 Hull Points, transport 10.
- Chaos Vindicator — Heavy Support.

## Conversion choices

- The 7th-edition unit identity, squad size and baseline points are the primary
  source for the new units.  The point adjustments are deliberately narrow:
  Scouts 13/15, Possessed 26/28, Plague Marines 24/27, Obliterators 75,
  Vindicators 125 and Land Raider 250.
- Project-native conventions take precedence: Astartes use 7-inch movement,
  the existing faction rules, and normal Marines remain at one Wound.  The
  2-Wound Centurion and Obliterator profiles are intentional heavy-infantry
  exceptions, comparable to the existing Terminator treatment.
- Vehicle armour, Hull Points, movement and weapon profiles follow the
  catalogue's existing Predator/Land Raider conventions, checked against the
  7th and Heresy references.
- Possessed use a predictable per-Fight-phase mutation roll rather than the
  full 7th-edition mutation-table sprawl.  This keeps them playable in the
  hybrid system and easy to revise after table testing.

## Bike and jetbike correction

The audit found a real cross-faction data error.  Chaos Bike Champions had
`Firstborn Astartes` and `Fury of the Firstborn`; these have been replaced with
`Discipline of the Long War`.  Standard Chaos Bikes and both Chaos Jetbike
profiles now have the expected Chaos faction rules and Firing Protocols (2),
matching the project-wide mounted-firepower convention without changing the
existing Heresy jetbike movement package.
