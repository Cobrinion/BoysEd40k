# Infiltrator Squad — completion review

- 5–10 models: 4–9 Infiltrators and one Sergeant, at 24 points per model.
- W1 Primaris baseline, matching the rest of this catalogue; Sergeant A3/Ld9.
- The standard loadout now visibly includes the shared Bolt Pistol and Close Combat Weapon, alongside the Marksman Bolt Carbine.
- One Infiltrator may replace its carbine with either a 10-point Helix Gauntlet or 5-point Infiltrator Comms Array; the choices are mutually exclusive.
- Unit rules are displayed only through the Unit profile's Special Rules characteristic. Concealed Positions and Omni-scrambler resolve to shared GST rules.

Closest Firstborn comparison: Scout/Vanguard reconnaissance units. Infiltrators pay above a standard Primaris body for forward deployment, reserve denial and one utility specialist, while remaining W1 and carrying no special or heavy weapon suite.
