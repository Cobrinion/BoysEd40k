## Balance comparison

The primary Firstborn comparator is the Assault Centurion: both are T5/W2, but a Centurion has a 2+ save, S5 and a much higher cost, while an Aggressor has a 3+ save, S4 and a much cheaper 30/35-point chassis. Aggressors gain short-range volume and S8/AP2 Unwieldy melee; Centurions remain the more durable dedicated elite platform. This makes Aggressors a strong value unit, not an underpowered one. Their current points are retained pending table testing rather than increased speculatively.
