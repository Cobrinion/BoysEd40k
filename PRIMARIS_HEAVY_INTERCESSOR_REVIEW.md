# Primaris Heavy Intercessor Squad review

## Completed pass

- Added the squad as a Troops choice: one required Sergeant and four to nine Heavy Intercessors.
- Gravis uses the established Boys Edition chassis: `M5 WS4 BS4 S4 T5 W2 I4 A2 Ld8 Sv3+`; the Sergeant has `A3 Ld9`.
- Every model has a bolt pistol, close combat weapon and frag/krak grenades. The displayed Primaris rules are Bolter Discipline, Combat Doctrines and The Codex Astartes.
- Squad rifle selection is mandatory and mutually exclusive: Heavy Bolt Rifle, Hellstorm Bolt Rifle or Executor Bolt Rifle.
- The matching heavy weapon is exposed only for its selected rifle family. A five to nine-model squad may take one; a ten-model squad may take two. Each costs 10 points.
- The squad costs 25 points per model: 125 points for five and 250 for ten.

## Balance comparison

At 25 points, a Heavy Intercessor is deliberately below a 30-point Aggressor: both have Gravis durability, but the Aggressor has assault-oriented wargear and substantially greater close-range output. Heavy Intercessors instead pay for resilient objective infantry and high-quality long-range bolt fire. They use the project&apos;s established two-Wound Gravis convention, not a modern three-Wound import.
