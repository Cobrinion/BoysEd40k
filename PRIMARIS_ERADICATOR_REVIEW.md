# Eradicator Squad — completion review

Revision12 reassessment supersedes the older points below:50/model (three150), +10 shared multi-melta (three with one160). Heavy-rifle surcharge removed as its native profile provides no offensive gain. Total Obliteration uses Run, not Advance. See PRIMARIS_EXECUTIONER_AND_ERADICATOR_REVIEW.md for comparison against actual Legion Heavy Support costs and explicit playtest limits.

## Source checked

`Codex (9e) - Space Marines (Errata 1.1).pdf`, Eradicator Squad (printed page 175) and points list (printed page 206).

## Implemented

- Three to six Gravis models at 40 points each, including exactly one Sergeant.
- Mandatory melta rifles or a source-legal all-squad replacement to heavy melta rifles, costed at +5 per model.
- Multi-meltas cost +10 and are limited dynamically to one per three models: one for 3–5 Eradicators and two for six.
- Total Obliteration is a shared rule; model cards use the Boys Edition Gravis T5/W2 baseline.

## Balance check

The primary Firstborn comparison is the multi-melta Devastator package. Eradicators pay 40 points per durable Gravis body for reliable short-range anti-tank fire, but their low movement, small maximum size and tightly constrained weapon list keep them distinct from flexible Devastator squads. Total Obliteration is powerful but requires a single target and no Advance.
