## Runtime validation matrix

Run these in New Recruit once the local catalogue is open:

| Card | Legal configuration | Illegal configuration that must be rejected |
|---|---|---|
| Tau Commander | Four combined hardpoints, e.g. two twin-linked weapons, or Coldstar plus up to two Support Systems | More than four hardpoints, e.g. two twin-linked weapons plus any other weapon/support; Coldstar plus a normal ranged weapon |
| Crisis Team | 1–9 suits, each with no more than three combined hardpoints, e.g. one twin-linked weapon plus one normal weapon/support, and no more than two drones | Ten suits; a twin-linked weapon plus two other hardpoint selections; or a third drone on one suit |
| Crisis Shas'vre | One Shas'vre replacement in a Crisis Team; up to three combined Ranged Weapon/Support System/Signature System selections and two drones | Two Shas'vre, four combined system selections, a twin-linked weapon plus two further systems, or a third drone |
| Crisis Bodyguards | 1–9 models, each with up to three combined hardpoints, including twin-linked choices, and two drones | Ten Bodyguards; a twin-linked weapon plus two other hardpoint selections; or a third drone |
| XV25 Stealth Team | 3–6 suits, no more than one fusion upgrade per three Shas'ui | Fewer than three suits or too many fusion upgrades |
| Ghostkeel | 1–3 Ghostkeels, one weapon-replacement choice | Simultaneous cyclic-ion and secondary-weapon replacement choices |
| Tactical Drones | 4–12 standalone drones at 14 points each | Fewer than four or more than twelve standalone drones |
| Sniper Drone Team | One Marksman/three MV71 drones, plus up to two Marksmen and six drones | A third added Marksman or seventh added MV71 drone |
| Hammerhead Gunship | 1–3 vehicles, each with one turret and one secondary fit | Four vehicles, two turrets, or two secondary fits on one vehicle |
| Sky Ray Gunship | 1–3 vehicles, each with one secondary fit | Four vehicles or two secondary fits on one vehicle |
| Longstrike's Hammerhead | One turret, one secondary fit and up to two seeker missiles | Both turrets, two secondary fits or a third seeker missile |
| Vespid Stingwings | 4–12 total models; leader replaces one Stingwing | Fewer than four or more than twelve total bodies, or a leader selected without the corresponding one-model reduction |
| Kroot Carnivores | 10–20 total models; one Shaper replaces one Carnivore, with up to three Krootox and ten Hounds | A Shaper on top of the full Carnivore maximum, a fourth Krootox, or an eleventh Hound |

| Ethereal | One optional Ethereal weapon, each optional equipment item no more than once, and up to two drones | Both Ethereal weapon choices or a third drone |
| Cadre Fireblade | Up to two drones | A third drone |
