# Judiciar — review note

Completed as an 85-point HQ with absolver bolt pistol, executioner relic blade, frag grenades and krak grenades.  The save is shown as `3+/4++ (melee)` because Blade Parry is a melee-only invulnerable save.

The source's fight-order effect is implemented as the shared `Tempormortis` rule.  The relic blade's wound-roll-of-6 mortal-wound rider is retained as the shared `Executioner's Strike` weapon rule; it is not duplicated in the unit card.  Its points and one-wound-line profile are a Boys Edition conversion, checked against the Lieutenant, Ancient and Firstborn character range, rather than a direct use of the 9th-edition datasheet profile.
