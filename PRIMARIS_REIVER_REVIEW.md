# Reiver Squad — completion review

## Source checked

`Codex (9e) - Space Marines (Errata 1.1).pdf`, Reiver Squad (printed page 154) and points list (printed page 204).

## Implemented

- 5–10 models: 4–9 Reivers and exactly one Reiver Sergeant, at 18 points per model.
- Boys Edition Tacticus profile convention: M7, T4, W1, 3+; the Sergeant has A3/Ld9 and other Reivers A2/Ld8.
- Default special issue bolt pistols and combat knives, or an all-squad replacement to bolt carbines.  The Sergeant’s extra combat knife is available only after the carbine loadout is selected.
- Per-model grapnel launchers and Reiver grav-chutes are capped exactly to the number of Reivers, including the Sergeant, at 2 points each. They remain optional; the cap does not force every model to take them.
- Terror Troops is a shared rule. Grapnel launchers and grav-chutes are shown as wargear cards rather than copied into the unit Rules area.

## Balance check

The closest Firstborn checks are Assault Squads (18 points) and Vanguard Veterans (19 points). Reivers trade their jump-pack access and broad veteran weapon list for 3+ armour, reliable light melee and their leadership aura. At W1 and 18 points they sit between those roles without exceeding Vanguard Veteran flexibility.

## Intentional Boys Edition divergence

The source uses 9th-edition M6/W2 profiles.  This card follows the established Boys Edition normal-Primaris convention of M7/W1 rather than importing the 9th-edition wound system.
