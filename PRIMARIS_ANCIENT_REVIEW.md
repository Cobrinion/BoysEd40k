# Primaris Ancient — review note

Completed as a 75-point HQ.  It has the project-standard Primaris character profile, bolt pistol, bolt rifle and frag/krak grenades.  Its Astartes Banner effect is a shared rule and appears only through the model's Special Rules characteristic.

The 9th-edition source card was used for its equipment and banner role; its profile has been converted to the established Boys Edition one-wound-line/Primaris HQ pattern rather than copied directly.  The cost sits between the 75-point Lieutenant and the 80-point Apothecary, reflecting a durable command-support role rather than a standalone combat character.
