# Primaris Outrider Squad review

## Completed pass

- Added the missing Fast Attack unit as the source's fixed three-bike squad: two Outriders and one Sergeant.
- Verified the 45-points-per-model source cost: the squad is 135 points.
- Used the established Bike convention (`M14 T5`, Hammer of Wrath, Jink, Relentless and Bulky (3)) and the permitted Boys Edition platform exception of two Wounds. The Sergeant is `A3 Ld9`; other Outriders are `A2 Ld8`.
- Added the complete base loadout: heavy bolt pistol, twin bolt rifle, Astartes chainsword, frag grenades and krak grenades. Twin bolt rifle is correctly represented as 30&quot;, S4, AP4, Rapid Fire 2.
- Added a reusable shared Devastating Charge rule: a charging Outrider gains +2 Attacks for that fight.

## Balance comparison

The fixed 135-point squad is a durable, fast melee unit rather than a Firstborn bike replacement: its two Wounds and Devastating Charge compensate for the absence of weapon upgrades, while it retains the project-standard Bike rules and avoids modern four-Wound durability.
