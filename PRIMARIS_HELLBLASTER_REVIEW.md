# Hellblaster Squad — completion review

- 5–10 models: 4–9 Hellblasters and one Sergeant, at 33 points per model.
- W1 Primaris baseline, with the Sergeant at A3/Ld9.
- The whole squad must select one plasma-incinerator pattern: standard, assault or heavy. Each has normal and supercharge profiles, with Gets Hot linked on the weapon selection rather than the Unit Rules area.
- The Sergeant may replace its Bolt Pistol with a 5-point Plasma Pistol.
- No raw unit-rule block is used: common unit rules appear only in the model profile's Special Rules characteristic.

Closest Firstborn comparison: Legion Heavy Support and plasma-equipped Tactical Marines. Hellblasters pay for a uniform AP2 plasma role and weapon-pattern flexibility, but remain W1 infantry with no heavy/special-weapon mix or Terminator resilience.
