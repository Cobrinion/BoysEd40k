# Firstborn vs Chaos Space Marines analytical balance test

Status: **baseline marine balance is healthy; test faction rules and elite platforms before changing points.**

## Scope and design assumption

This test explicitly treats the one-Wound marine standard as intentional. The Firstborn and Chaos catalogues are built from a 7th Edition / Horus Heresy 1e / Horus Heresy 2e hybrid, not modern 40k&apos;s two-Wound marine baseline.

The comparison uses the current catalogue entries without Legion traits, Mark-specific bonuses, psychic powers, reactions, cover, characters, or formation rules unless named below.

## Baseline marine comparison

| Characteristic | Firstborn tactical marine | Chaos Space Marine | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Movement | 7&quot; | 7&quot; | Equal |
| WS / BS | 4 / 4 | 4 / 4 | Equal |
| S / T / W | 4 / 4 / 1 | 4 / 4 / 1 | Equal |
| I / A / Ld / Save | 4 / 1 / 8 / 3+ | 4 / 1 / 8 / 3+ | Equal |
| Stationary bolter rule | Fury of the Firstborn | Discipline of the Long War | Same rule text: +1 bolter shot if the model did not move or Run |

At a stationary rapid-fire exchange, ten generic Firstborn and ten generic Chaos Marines each inflict about 2.22 unsaved wounds on the other before faction buffs. At normal single-shot range, both inflict about 1.11. The core line-marine exchange is mathematically symmetrical.

## Faction-rule comparison

| Faction | Rule | Balance value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Firstborn | Firstborn Astartes | Always attempts Regroup at normal Ld regardless of casualties, plus future Legion gene-seed benefits. Reliable defensive value. |
| Chaos | Warbands of Chaos | Champion casualty causes D3 S4 AP- infighting hits, then a replacement identical basic model. Swingy, and potentially a liability. |
| Chaos characters | Champion of Chaos | Must issue/accept challenges; gains a Chaos boon after killing a character. High ceiling, with matchup and challenge risk. |

Initial conclusion: Firstborn has the more reliable army-wide baseline. Chaos has greater variance and character upside. This is a good asymmetry if the unit costs remain close; it should not be "corrected" by giving either faction extra Wounds.

## Risks to test before points changes

1. **Legion traits versus Chaos Marks/Warband mechanics.** Generic statlines are even; special rules may not be.
2. **Characters in challenges.** Champion of Chaos can become either a tax or a snowball effect depending on how often character duels occur.
3. **Contemptor versus Helbrute loadouts.** Base profiles are reasonably priced apart, but weapon access can erase or magnify the 50-point gap.
4. **Veterans, Chosen, Berzerkers, Noise Marines, and specialist Legion units.** These should be tested as separate archetypes, not treated as generic marines.

## Recommended matched games

Use 1,500-point games with matched missions/terrain and swap first turn:

1. Generic Firstborn tactical/rhino/predator core versus Chaos Marine/rhino/predator/havoc core.
2. Firstborn Captain, Terminators, and Contemptor versus Chaos Lord, Terminators, and Helbrute.
3. One Legion-trait list versus one Chaos Mark/Warband list, keeping the rest of the force as generic as possible.

Record unit losses, challenge outcomes, successful Regroup tests, Warband casualty events, and VP differential. Do not adjust base marine Wounds or profiles unless repeated games show a systemic problem.
