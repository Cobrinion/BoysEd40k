# Bladeguard Veteran Squad — completion review

Source: Space Marines 9th-edition Codex, printed page 150 and points page 203.

- 3–6 Elite Veterans at 35 points each, including one Sergeant (two Veterans plus the Sergeant at minimum).
- Every model has a master-crafted power sword, heavy bolt pistol, storm shield and grenades; the Sergeant may replace its pistol with a 5-point neo-volkite or plasma pistol.
- Boys Edition uses the normal Primaris W1 baseline. The shared Storm Shield is represented in the profile as 2+/3++. The squad links to the existing Master-Crafted Power Sword rather than defining a duplicate weapon profile.

Closest Firstborn comparison: Vanguard Veterans / Terminators. Bladeguard trade Terminators' second Wound, AP2 weapon access, ranged/heavy options and Terminator-armour special rules for 7&quot; movement, a 3++ save and extra basic attacks. Their intended role is resilient AP3 melee, not a replacement for Terminators.
